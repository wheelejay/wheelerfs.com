Your HACCP plan, storage logs, and cook records are only as good as the instruments behind them. We check each thermometer and humidity meter against calibrated reference standards and give you a certificate showing exactly how accurate it is.

## What we verify

- **Thermohygrometers** (temperature and humidity meters), such as the ones in dry storage and warehouses
- **Removable temperature probes**, such as handheld and cook probes
- **Fixed temperature gauges** on kettles, ovens, cookers, and other equipment, checked in place
- **Fridge, freezer, and room thermometers**

## How it works

- **Thermohygrometers** are checked at two humidity points, ambient and about 75 %RH, in a sealed salt-solution chamber. Temperature is checked at each point.
- **Removable probes** are checked at the ice point (0 °C / 32 °F) and at your operating temperature, such as your cook temperature.
- **Fixed gauges** are checked in place, at the equipment's operating temperature.
- **Fridge, freezer, and room thermometers** are checked in place, with the reference beside the device.

Every reading is compared with our reference standard. The certificate shows the reference reading, your device's reading, the error, and pass or fail against the manufacturer's tolerance or your own.

## If a device is out of tolerance

If a device can be adjusted, we adjust it and check it again, and the certificate becomes a Certificate of Calibration showing the readings before and after. If it can't be brought back into tolerance, the certificate says so, and we let you know right away so you can tag it out of service.

## What you get

- **A certificate for each device** with its serial number, location, test points, results, and measurement uncertainty
- **NIST-traceable reference standards**, calibrated by an accredited laboratory
- **A QR code** on each certificate that anyone can scan to confirm it's genuine
- **Online access** to every certificate in your customer portal
