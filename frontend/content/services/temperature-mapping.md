Temperatures vary from one part of a room to another. Temperature mapping finds the hot and cold spots in your storage areas and equipment, so you know where product is safe to store and where to put your monitoring sensors.

## What's included

- **Mapping of ambient and cold storage rooms**, freezers, and high-temperature equipment
- **Validated data loggers** placed throughout the space
- **Identification of hot and cold spots** and recommendations for sensor placement
- **A full comprehensive report** for your records and auditors

## When to map

- When a room or piece of equipment is new or modified
- After changes to racking, layout, or refrigeration equipment
- Periodically, as your food safety plan or customers require
- When temperature excursions or complaints point to a problem

## Pricing

Every space is different, so temperature mapping is quoted per project based on the size and type of space, the number of loggers needed, and how long the study runs. [Contact us](/#contact) with a few details about what you need mapped and we'll send you a quote.
