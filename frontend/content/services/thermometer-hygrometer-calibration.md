Your HACCP plan, storage logs, and cook records are only as good as the instruments behind them. We calibrate each thermometer and humidity meter against NIST-traceable reference standards and give you a Certificate of Calibration showing exactly how accurate it is.

## What we calibrate

- **Thermohygrometers** (temperature and humidity meters), such as the ones in dry storage and warehouses
- **Removable temperature probes**, such as handheld and cook probes
- **Fixed temperature gauges** on kettles, ovens, cookers, and other equipment, calibrated in place
- **Fridge, freezer, and room thermometers**

## How it works

- **Thermohygrometers** are checked at two humidity points, ambient and about 75 %RH, in a sealed salt-solution chamber. Temperature is checked at each point.
- **Removable probes** are checked at the ice point (0 °C / 32 °F) and at your operating temperature, such as your cook temperature.
- **Fixed gauges** are checked in place, at the equipment's operating temperature.
- **Fridge, freezer, and room thermometers** are checked in place, with the reference beside the device.

Every reading is compared with our reference standard and the error is recorded. Each point also gets a pass or fail against the manufacturer's tolerance or your own, so the certificate covers verification too.

## Calibration doesn't mean adjustment

A calibration is the comparison against a traceable reference and the documented error. It doesn't require adjusting your device. If a device is out of tolerance and can be adjusted, we adjust it and check it again, and the certificate shows both the as-found and as-left readings. If it can't be brought back into tolerance, the certificate says so, and we let you know right away so you can tag it out of service.

## What you get

- **A Certificate of Calibration for each device** with its serial number, location, test points, error, pass/fail results, and measurement uncertainty
- **NIST-traceable reference standards**, calibrated by an accredited laboratory
- **A QR code** on each certificate that anyone can scan to confirm it's genuine
- **Online access** to every certificate in your customer portal
