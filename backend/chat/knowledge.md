## Services

### Metal Detector Validation
Page: https://wheelerfs.com/services/metal-detector-validation
- Sensitivity testing with certified ferrous, non-ferrous, and stainless steel
  test samples.
- Testing in the customer's real products, including hardest-to-inspect ones,
  so product effect is accounted for.
- Worst-case positioning (test samples through the center of the aperture).
- Reject system checks at normal line speed, including reject timing and
  confirmation.
- Fail-safe checks for alarms and belt stops where the system has them.
- A full audit-ready report for each unit, suitable for FDA, USDA, SQF, and
  BRCGS audits.
- Price: $650 for the first unit, $250 for each additional unit validated on
  the same visit.

### X-ray System Validation
Page: https://wheelerfs.com/services/x-ray-system-validation
- Most customers use X-ray to find metal, often in packaging or products where
  a metal detector struggles (foil packs, metallized film).
- Metal detectability testing using certified ferrous, non-ferrous, and
  stainless steel test samples.
- Testing in the customer's products and packaging.
- Reject system checks.
- Traceable documentation and a comprehensive validation report per system.
- Other contaminants (glass, stone, bone): Wheeler Food Safety can source the
  certified test standards a customer's program requires. Mention it when
  requesting a quote.
- Price: $700 for the first unit, $300 for each additional system validated on
  the same visit.

### Magnet Validation
Page: https://wheelerfs.com/services/magnet-validation
- Visual inspection for damage, wear, and proper installation.
- Magnetic strength testing.
- Capture efficiency testing.
- A full detailed report.
- Price: $350 for the first unit, $150 for each additional unit validated on
  the same visit.

### Temperature Mapping
Page: https://wheelerfs.com/services/temperature-mapping
- Mapping of ambient and cold storage rooms, coolers, freezers, warehouses,
  and high-temperature equipment such as ovens.
- Validated data loggers placed throughout the space.
- Identification of hot and cold spots and recommendations for sensor
  placement.
- A comprehensive report.
- Price: quoted per project, based on the size and type of space, the number
  of loggers, and the length of the study. There is no fixed price; offer to
  collect details for a quote.

## How multi-unit visits are billed
Metal detectors, X-ray systems, and magnets validated on the same visit are
billed together:
- The single most expensive unit on the visit is billed at its first-unit
  price (an X-ray system if there is one, otherwise a metal detector,
  otherwise a magnet).
- Every other unit, of any type, is billed at that type's additional-unit
  price (X-ray $300, metal detector $250, magnet $150).
- Example: 2 X-ray systems and 3 metal detectors = $700 + $300 + 3 x $250 =
  $1,750.
- Temperature mapping is quoted separately.
Use the estimate_price tool for any calculation.

## General guidance customers often ask about
- Equipment is typically validated at least once a year, and again after
  installation or relocation, major repairs or software updates, new products
  or packaging, line speed changes, or when a customer or certification body
  requires it.
- Annual validation is different from the operators' daily verification checks
  (start-up, during production, shutdown), which the plant does itself.
