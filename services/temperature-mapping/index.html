Temperature Mapping

Temperature mapping services for refrigerators, coolers, freezers, warehouses, and ovens in Utah, with data loggers throughout the space and a full report of hot and cold spots.

Temperatures vary from one part of a room to another. Temperature mapping finds the hot and cold spots in your storage areas and equipment, so you know where product is safe to store and where to put your monitoring sensors.

What we map

  • Walk-in coolers and refrigerators, where a few degrees can decide whether product stays safe
  • Freezers and blast freezers, including the areas near doors and evaporators that drift during defrost and loading
  • Warehouses and dry storage, ambient or temperature-controlled, where the top racks and exterior walls often run warmest
  • Ovens and hot-holding equipment, where cold spots can leave product undercooked

What's included

  • Mapping of ambient and cold storage rooms, freezers, and high-temperature equipment
  • Validated data loggers placed throughout the space
  • Identification of hot and cold spots and recommendations for sensor placement
  • A full comprehensive report for your records and auditors

When to map

  • When a room or piece of equipment is new or modified
  • After changes to racking, layout, or refrigeration equipment
  • Periodically, as your food safety plan or customers require
  • When temperature excursions or complaints point to a problem

How a mapping study works

  1. Plan: we review the size, layout, racking, doors, and refrigeration of the space, and how product is stored.
  2. Place loggers: data loggers go in a three-dimensional grid, high, middle, and low, with extra loggers near doors, docks, and evaporators.
  3. Record: the loggers record over normal operations, so door openings, defrost cycles, and loading are captured.
  4. Report: you get the warmest and coldest locations, the temperature spread, any readings outside your range, and where to put your monitoring sensors.
