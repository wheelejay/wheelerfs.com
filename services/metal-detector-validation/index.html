Metal Detector Validation

Independent metal detector validation for Utah food manufacturers, with certified ferrous, non-ferrous, and stainless steel testing and an audit-ready report for every unit.

Your metal detector is a critical control, and auditors want proof that it works on the products you actually run. We test each detector the way an auditor expects and give you a clear, defensible report.

What's included

  • Sensitivity testing with certified ferrous, non-ferrous, and stainless steel test samples
  • Testing in your real products, including the hardest-to-inspect ones, so product effect is accounted for
  • Worst-case positioning, passing test samples through the center of the aperture
  • Reject system checks at normal line speed, including reject timing and confirmation
  • Fail-safe checks for alarms and belt stops where your system has them
  • Adjustment and retesting if something fails, until the detector passes
  • A full audit-ready report for each unit, ready for FDA, USDA, SQF, and BRCGS audits

When to validate

  • At least once a year
  • When a detector is installed or moved
  • After major repairs, part replacements, or software updates
  • When you add a new product, package type, or line speed
  • When a customer or certification body requires it

Looking for metal detector calibration?

Many plants ask for "metal detector calibration", and some providers sell it under that name. What auditors actually look for is validation: proof that the detector finds and rejects the test piece sizes your food safety plan specifies, in your real products. Our validation includes the calibration work too. If a detector fails, we adjust its settings and retest until it passes, and the report records both the as-found and as-left settings. So if your auditor or customer asked for a metal detector calibration, this is the service that covers it, with the documentation to prove it.
